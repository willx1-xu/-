Why Apex

Manufacturing and Project Support Buyers Can Evaluate

Headquartered in Nanjing, Jiangsu, Apex Power Systems helps international customers connect power distribution equipment with intelligent EV charging systems.

Apex Power Systems Co., Ltd. specializes in integrated power distribution and EV charging infrastructure solutions. Our product portfolio includes power transformers, compact substations, HV/LV switchgear, EV chargers, and intelligent charging station controllers with OCPP 1.6J/2.0 readiness.

By combining grid-side electrical equipment and charger-side intelligent control into a unified architecture, we help customers reduce multi-vendor coordination risks and build more reliable charging and power infrastructure.

We support OEM/ODM cooperation, project-based configuration, and export-oriented technical documentation for overseas customers.
