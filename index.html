About Apex Power Systems

China-Based Manufacturer Focused on Integrated Grid-to-Plug Solutions

Headquartered in Nanjing, Jiangsu, Apex Power Systems helps international customers connect power distribution equipment with intelligent EV charging systems.

Apex Power Systems Co., Ltd. specializes in integrated power distribution and EV charging infrastructure solutions. Our product portfolio includes power transformers, prefabricated substations, MV/LV switchgear, DC fast charging piles, and intelligent charging station controllers with OCPP 1.6J/2.0 readiness.

By combining grid-side electrical equipment and charger-side intelligent control into a unified architecture, we help customers reduce multi-vendor coordination risks and build more reliable charging and power infrastructure.

We support OEM/ODM cooperation, IEC-compliant product design, project-based configuration, and export-oriented technical documentation for customers in North America, Europe, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and South America.
