## Internal Linking Strategy

Use a hub-and-cluster structure when future pages and edits are approved:

1. Treat `/solutions/ev-charging-station-power-solution/` as the primary EV
   charging system hub. Link it to charging-pile, charging-substation,
   transformer, switchgear and controller product pages.
2. Treat `/solutions/industrial-plant-power-system/` as the power distribution
   hub. Link it to transformer, KYN28-12 MV switchgear, GCS/GGD and XL cabinet
   pages.
3. Link every new guide to one principal solution page and two to four
   closely relevant product pages using descriptive anchor text, such as
   `10kV box-type substation` rather than `learn more`.
4. Add reciprocal contextual links from enhanced product and solution pages
   back to genuinely helpful guides; do not add unrelated sitewide guide links
   merely to increase link counts.
5. Use `/resources/` as the index for all published guides and documentation,
   linking to guides by topic cluster: charging systems, transformers,
   switchgear and controls.
6. Link trust and conversion content where it assists evaluation: guides can
   point to `/quality/`, `/certificates/`, `/projects/` and `/contact/` when
   relevant.
7. End guides with a restrained RFQ route using the existing contact details:
   `xuke@link-jl.com` and `+86 13201571341`.
