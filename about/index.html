About Apex Power Systems

Integrated Charging Power Solutions

Apex Power Systems Co., Ltd. is a China-based manufacturer specializing in EV charging infrastructure solutions, headquartered in Nanjing, Jiangsu, China.

Contact Apex
Company Profile

From Grid Power to Vehicle Charging

Apex provides end-to-end Charging solutions that connect high-voltage power distribution equipment with intelligent EV charging systems. Our product portfolio includes AC/DC charging piles, group charging systems, and DC fast charging piles, AC charging piles, and intelligent charging station controllers with OCPP 1.6J / 2.0 readiness.

By combining power distribution equipment and EV charging technologies into a unified system architecture, Apex helps customers reduce multi-vendor coordination risks and build more reliable charging and power infrastructure.
