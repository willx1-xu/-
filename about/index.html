About Apex Power Systems

Integrated Grid-to-Plug Power Solutions

Apex Power Systems Co., Ltd. is a China-based manufacturer specializing in integrated power distribution and EV charging infrastructure solutions, headquartered in Nanjing, Jiangsu, China.

Contact Apex
Company Profile

From Grid Power to Vehicle Charging

Apex provides end-to-end Grid-to-Plug solutions that connect high-voltage power distribution equipment with intelligent EV charging systems. Our product portfolio includes power transformers, prefabricated substations, MV/LV switchgear, DC fast charging piles, AC charging piles, and intelligent charging station controllers with OCPP 1.6J / 2.0 readiness.

By combining power distribution equipment and EV charging technologies into a unified system architecture, Apex helps customers reduce multi-vendor coordination risks and build more reliable charging and power infrastructure.
