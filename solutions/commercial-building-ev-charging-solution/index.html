Overview

Solution Overview

Apex Commercial Building + EV Charging Solution helps hotels, office buildings, shopping malls, parking facilities, and mixed-use properties add reliable power distribution and EV charging capability.

The solution can include dry-type transformers, LV distribution cabinets, AC charging piles, DC fast chargers, and intelligent charging station controllers.
