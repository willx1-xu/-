Overview

Solution Overview

The Apex OEM / ODM Charging Equipment Solution is built for charging pile manufacturers, station integrators, distributors and technology partners. It provides intelligent charging controllers, OCPP and CHAdeMO protocol boards, and product customisation to accelerate branded charger development.

How the System Works

1

2

Select JC-6512, JC-6513, JC-6650 or protocol boards according to the charger system.

3

Protocol Adaptation

Support OCPP/CHAdeMO network communication and project-based integration.

4

OEM/ODM Customisation

5

Batch Support
