Overview

Solution Overview

Apex OEM / ODM Charging Equipment Solution is built for charging pile manufacturers, charging station integrators, distributors, and technology partners who need customized charging hardware and control solutions.

The solution includes intelligent charging controllers, OCPP protocol conversion boards, CHAdeMO protocol boards, AC/DC charging control, and product customization support.
