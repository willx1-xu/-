Overview

Solution Overview

Apex EV Charging Station Power Solution connects grid-side power distribution equipment with charger-side intelligent control to support public, commercial, fleet, and heavy-duty charging station projects.

Instead of purchasing transformers, cabinets, chargers, and controllers from multiple vendors, customers can build a more unified Charging architecture with Apex.

System Architecture

How the System Works

1

Grid Intake

Utility power is connected through project-based medium-voltage distribution design.

2

Charging / Charging

A charger or prefabricated charging system converts and distributes power for charging loads.

3

LV Distribution

Charging piles and distribution equipment protect and supply power to charging terminals.

4

Charging Equipment

AC or DC fast charging piles are selected according to station capacity and vehicle type.

5

Intelligent Control

OCPP-ready controllers support networked charging operation and system integration.
