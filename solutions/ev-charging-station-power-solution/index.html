Overview

Solution Overview

Apex EV Charging Station Power Solution connects grid-side power distribution equipment with charger-side intelligent control to support public, commercial, fleet, and heavy-duty charging station projects.

Instead of purchasing transformers, cabinets, chargers, and controllers from multiple vendors, customers can build a more unified Grid-to-Plug architecture with Apex.

System Architecture

How the System Works

1

Grid Intake

Utility power is connected through project-based medium-voltage distribution design.

2

Transformer / Substation

A transformer or prefabricated substation converts and distributes power for charging loads.

3

LV Distribution

LV switchgear and distribution cabinets protect and distribute power to charging terminals.

4

Charging Equipment

AC or DC fast charging piles are selected according to station capacity and vehicle type.

5

Intelligent Control

OCPP-ready controllers support networked charging operation and system integration.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What equipment is needed for an EV charging station power system?

Typical equipment includes transformer or compact substation, LV switchgear, distribution cabinet, DC fast chargers or AC chargers, charging controllers, cables, protection devices, and monitoring systems.

Can Apex provide both power equipment and charging equipment?

Yes. Apex provides power transformers, substations, switchgear, DC charging piles, AC charging piles, and intelligent charging controllers.

What information is needed for a charging station solution?

Please provide project country, grid voltage, planned charging power, number of chargers, vehicle type, site layout, and communication requirements.
