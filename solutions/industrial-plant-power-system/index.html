Overview

Solution Overview

Apex Industrial Plant Power System is designed for factories, mines, processing plants, logistics parks, and heavy-duty industrial facilities that need reliable electrical distribution equipment.

The solution can combine oil-immersed or dry-type transformers, MV switchgear, LV switchgear, power cabinets, and project-based protection design.

System Architecture

How the System Works

1

Utility Input

Medium-voltage power enters the plant electrical system.

2

Transformer

Transformer converts grid voltage to plant distribution voltage.

3

MV Protection

KYN28-12 MV switchgear supports control and protection.

4

LV Distribution

GCS, GGD, or XL cabinets distribute power to equipment and production lines.

5

Load Management

Project-based configuration supports industrial load, motor, and facility requirements.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What equipment is normally used for industrial plant power distribution?

Typical equipment includes transformer, MV switchgear, LV switchgear, distribution cabinets, control cabinets, protection units, and cables.

Can Apex customize the voltage and cabinet arrangement?

Yes. Voltage, capacity, cabinet layout, current rating, protection scheme, and enclosure configuration can be customized.

Which transformer type should an industrial plant use?

It depends on installation location, capacity, fire safety requirements, environment, and project standard. Apex can help select oil-immersed or dry-type transformer options.
