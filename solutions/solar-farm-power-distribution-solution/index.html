Overview

Solution Overview

Apex Solar Farm Power Distribution Solution supports renewable energy projects that require reliable power transformation, protection, and grid connection.

The solution can include oil-immersed transformers, compact substations, KYN28-12 MV switchgear, LV cabinets, and project-based protection configuration.

System Architecture

How the System Works

1

PV Array Output

Solar power generation is collected through project-side electrical infrastructure.

2

Step-Up Transformer

Transformer converts voltage according to grid connection requirements.

3

Compact Substation

Prefabricated substation integrates transformer, switchgear, and protection systems.

4

MV Switchgear

Medium-voltage switchgear supports control, protection, metering, and grid connection.

5

Grid Connection

Final configuration is adapted to local utility and project requirements.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Apex provide transformer and switchgear for solar farms?

Yes. Apex can supply transformers, compact substations, MV switchgear, LV cabinets, and related power distribution equipment.

What should I provide for solar project quotation?

Please provide project capacity, grid voltage, transformer capacity, single-line diagram, environmental conditions, quantity, and destination country.

Can the compact substation be customized for solar projects?

Yes. Layout, voltage, capacity, enclosure, protection level, and internal configuration can be customized.
